Mindheim

Free learning apps for grades 1–8. Maths, geometry, physics, algebra, spelling, chemistry and electricity, each explained step by step in its own small app that lives on your device.

Visit mindheim.app ↗ See the apps ↓

One subject, one app

Each app is a small installable web app on its own subdomain of mindheim.app. Install the ones you need, or all of them: they share the same look, the same owl and the same privacy promise.

What every app shares

The apps are separate, but they're built on one engine and one UI library, so they behave the same way everywhere.
